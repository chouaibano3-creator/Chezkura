# Chez Kura — Brand Guide

## Positioning

**What it's about:** the feeling of walking into a home and being instantly at
peace. The house. The family. The details that make a space feel like yours.

**Who it's for:** two overlapping buyers —
- Younger and mid-age buyers furnishing for aesthetics (Pinterest/TikTok-driven,
  wants the room to *look* right)
- Older buyers investing in their home for comfort and permanence

**Why pets are in the brand:** a pet is part of what makes a house feel like a
home. The pet collection is an extension of the same feeling, not a second
business. It sits below Home everywhere it appears.

**What makes it defensible:** every piece is made to order for one customer.
Nothing is warehoused, nothing is repeated. That removes direct price comparison
(no one else sells the identical item), supports premium pricing, and means
returns are legitimately final sale.

---

## Naming

- Always write the brand as **Chez Kura** — both words, together.
- Never shorten to "Kura" alone in handles, domains, or ad copy. IKEA has a
  well-known KURA product line; using the full name avoids the search collision.
- Domain: `chezkurahome.com` (the `.com` for the bare name was already taken;
  adding "home" also reinforces the positioning).

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bone | `#F6F1EA` | Page background, primary surface |
| Sand | `#E3D5C3` | Secondary surface, hero gradient, promise row |
| Terracotta | `#C17A56` | Primary accent, buttons, **Home** collection |
| Sage | `#93A084` | Secondary accent, **Pets** collection |
| Charcoal | `#2B2723` | Text, dark bands, footer |

Supporting inks: `#5C574F` (soft body text), `#8A8579` (muted labels),
`rgba(43,39,35,0.10)` (hairline borders).

**Rule:** terracotta marks Home, sage marks Pets. That single distinction is how
two collections read as one brand.

---

## Typography

| Level | Face | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Geometric sans — Futura, Poppins, Neue Montreal | Bold, `.22em` letter-spacing |
| Headings | Same geometric sans | Bold, tight tracking (`-0.01em`) |
| Eyebrow labels | Same | 11–12px, uppercase, `.28em` spacing |
| Body / product copy | Humanist sans — Inter, Work Sans | Regular, 1.6 line-height |

No serif or display faces anywhere.

---

## Logo

Wordmark `CHEZ KURA` in wide-tracked geometric sans, preceded by a simple open
arch mark — a single terracotta stroke suggesting a roofline or doorway. Never
literal (no house icon, no roof triangle, no paw print).

---

## Voice

Warm, calm, a little poetic. Never salesy.

**Do:**
- Short sentences. Room to breathe.
- Speak about the home and the pet with the same tenderness.
- "Made only for your space." "Made once, for one home. Yours."

**Don't:**
- Exclamation marks, all-caps urgency, countdown timers, fake scarcity.
- The words AI, algorithm, generated, automated — anywhere customer-facing.
- Feature-listing before feeling. Lead with the feeling.

**Tagline:** *Every piece made for your space, or for the one who never leaves
your side.*

## Product architecture

**Chez Kura · Home** (primary — terracotta)
Cushion covers · Wall art & framed prints · Rugs & mats

**Chez Kura · Pets** (secondary — sage)
Pet portrait canvases · Pet name blankets · Photo mats

Pet items require a customer photo upload and carry a longer lead time. Say so
plainly on the product page — the wait is part of the value, not an apology.
